# RedGIFs for iOS

A lightweight, native iOS experience for browsing and watching RedGIFs.

Discover trending content, explore categories, search for creators, and enjoy animated media through a clean, streamlined interface designed specifically for iPhone.

### Features

- **Discover** — Explore trending, popular, and newly uploaded content.
- **Search** — Find GIFs, videos, tags, and creators.
- **Creator Profiles** — Explore creator pages and their published content.
- **Favorites/Library** — Keep track of content you want to revisit.
- **Native Experience** — An intuitive interface optimized for iOS.

### What's not there

- **Login** — There's a chance of accounts getting restricted/suspended when using unofficial clients. This also saves me the time and headache of having to maintain authenticated parts of the code.
- **Downloads** — As-per RedGifs terms.


### About

RedGIFs brings the RedGIFs browsing experience to iOS in a compact, purpose-built application.
